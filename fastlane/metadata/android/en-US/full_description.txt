See your New Zealand bank accounts, spending, and transactions in one calm, private dashboard.

Fern is a personal finance and money-tracking app for New Zealand. Connect eligible bank accounts through Akahu to view balances, your overall position, recent activity, spending categories, and merchants in one place.

UNDERSTAND YOUR MONEY

• View account balances and your overall net position
• Browse and search recent transactions
• Explore spending breakdowns, trends, categories, and top merchants
• Automatically categorise common New Zealand merchants
• Create your own category overrides and reusable rules
• Attach receipt images to transactions
• Export and import your data

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Fern connects directly to Akahu using the access token and app token you provide. Your credentials and financial data are stored locally on your device. Fern has no developer-operated server, advertising, analytics, or data sales.

TRY IT FIRST

Use Explore demo to browse the complete dashboard with sample data before connecting an account.
