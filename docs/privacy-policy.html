Fern Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 August 2026

Fern is a personal-finance app for New Zealand bank accounts. This policy explains how Fern handles your information.

Information Fern handles

When you connect Fern, you provide an Akahu user access token and app ID token. Fern uses those tokens to retrieve the bank-account and transaction information that you have authorised Akahu to provide. This can include account names, balances, account numbers, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, merchant information, and spending categories.

Fern also handles preferences and content you choose to add, such as categorisation rules and receipt images. You can choose to export or import your Fern data using files on your device.

How information is used and shared

Fern uses this information only to provide its personal-finance features, including displaying balances and transactions, categorising spending, searching activity, and generating statistics.

Fern communicates directly with Akahu's API to retrieve information you have authorised. Your information is not sent to Fern's developer or sold to third parties. Fern does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Storage and security

Your Akahu tokens are stored in your device's secure credential storage. Bank and transaction information, preferences, categorisation rules, and receipt images are stored locally on your device. Network requests to Akahu use encrypted HTTPS connections.

You are responsible for keeping any exported files secure. Their handling after export is controlled by you and the apps or storage locations you select.

Retention and deletion

Fern keeps locally stored information until you remove it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. You can sign out in Fern's settings to revoke the Akahu user access token and remove Fern's access. You can also manage or revoke access through Akahu. Clearing Fern's app data or uninstalling Fern removes information stored locally by the app, subject to your device's backup settings.

Children

Fern is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Fern's features or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
